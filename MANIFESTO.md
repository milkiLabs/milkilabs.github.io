# The Milki Labs Manifesto

## Why Milki Labs Exists

Modern productivity software promised clarity, efficiency, and freedom.  
Instead, it delivered subscriptions, surveillance, lock-in, and dependency.

Your tasks live on servers you don’t control.  
Your notes are mined for engagement.  
Your focus is treated as a resource to be extracted.

**Milki Labs exists to reject this model.**

We believe productivity tools should help people live better lives —  
not turn their lives into products.

---

## Our Belief

Your data is not a feature.  
Your attention is not a metric.  
Your life is not SaaS.

What you create, plan, and organize belongs to **you**.

---

## Our Principles

### 1. User Ownership Is Non-Negotiable

All data created with Milki Labs tools belongs to the user.

- Users choose where their data is stored
- Users can export, inspect, or delete it at any time
- No hidden servers, no proprietary formats, no hostage data

Ownership is not a checkbox — it is the foundation.

---

### 2. Local-First by Default

Milki Labs software works **offline first**.

- Network access is optional
- Apps remain fully functional without connectivity
- Sync enhances the experience, but never controls it

If the network disappears, your tools should not.

---

### 3. Sync Without Surveillance

When synchronization is needed, it must respect autonomy.

- No centralized accounts
- No mandatory cloud
- No vendor-controlled backends

We use user-controlled storage and open protocols  
so devices can cooperate without corporations mediating.

---

### 4. Free as in Freedom

Milki Labs software is free and open source.

- Transparent by design
- Auditable by anyone
- Forkable by default

Open source is not a marketing label —  
it is a commitment to user sovereignty.

---

### 5. Simplicity Over Scale

We do not chase growth for its own sake.

- Fewer features, thoughtfully designed
- Calm interfaces, not addictive loops
- Tools that respect focus instead of fragmenting it

Complexity should serve people — not business models.

---

### 6. No Dark Patterns. Ever.

We do not manipulate behavior.

- No engagement traps
- No artificial friction
- No data-driven coercion

Trust is earned by restraint, not persuasion.

---

## What We Build

Milki Labs builds **personal productivity tools**, including but not limited to:

- Task management
- Notes and knowledge systems
- Planning and reflection tools

Each tool stands on its own,  
yet shares a common philosophy:  
**local-first, user-owned, open.**

---

## What We Refuse to Become

We will not become:
- An ad company
- A data broker
- A closed platform
- A surveillance layer disguised as productivity

If a feature requires betraying these principles,  
we choose not to build it.

---

## Our Measure of Success

Success is not measured in:
- Monthly active users
- Retention curves
- Venture rounds

Success is measured when:
- Users trust the tool without reading a privacy policy
- Their data outlives devices and companies
- The software can be forked and survive without us
